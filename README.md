# Customer & Product Analytics Dashboard

## Project Overview

An end-to-end e-commerce analytics project analyzing 25,000
customer sessions to understand revenue performance, customer
behavior, product performance, purchasing patterns, and cart
abandonment.

## Business Objectives

- Analyze overall revenue and purchasing performance
- Understand customer segments and repeat-session behavior
- Identify high-value customers
- Analyze product and category performance
- Examine discount and rating patterns
- Identify cart abandonment and conversion opportunities
- Develop actionable business insights

## Dataset

Indian E-Commerce Customer Behavior & Purchase dataset.

- 25,000 sessions
- 8,442 unique customers
- 899 unique products
- Data period: January–December 2024

## Tools Used

- Python
- Pandas
- MySQL
- Excel
- Power BI
- DAX

## Project Workflow

Python → Data Cleaning & EDA
        ↓
MySQL → Business Analysis
        ↓
Excel → Pivot Analysis
        ↓
Power BI → Interactive Dashboard
        ↓
Business Insights

## Key Findings

- Total revenue: ₹10.12M
- Purchase rate: 22.46%
- Cart abandonment rate: 65.15%
- Average order value: ₹1,801
- High-value customers contributed approximately 78% of revenue
- Repeat-session customers contributed approximately ₹9.55M
- Revenue varied substantially across product categories
